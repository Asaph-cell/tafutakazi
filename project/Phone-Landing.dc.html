Why Tafutakazi exists

Every job ad asks for experience. But you can't get experience without a job.

So capable graduates stay unemployed, not because they can't do the work, but because no one gives them a chance to show it. Tafutakazi exists to help reduce graduate unemployment. Here, companies post short tasks from real jobs, graduates do them, and employers hire based on what you can actually do, not on years of experience.

How it works

Pick a taskA real problem from a Kenyan company, done in a few hours.
Do it, then explain itYour work, a 2–3 minute video, and how you did it.
Get seenRecruiters watch, comment and invite the best to interview.
Your work stays privateOnly signed-in recruiters see it.
Real companies onlyNo one may ever ask you for money.
