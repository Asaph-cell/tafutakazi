Tafutakazi is free. For graduates and for recruiters.

It exists to help reduce graduate unemployment in Kenya. Employers ask for experience that fresh graduates can't get without a job, so Tafutakazi gives them a chance to prove what they can do. If it helped you, or you believe in it, here's how to keep it going.
